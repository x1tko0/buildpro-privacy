BuildPro

Material Estimator — Privacy Policy

Short version

Nothing leaves your phone. BuildPro has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not even have internet permission.

Data the app stores

The dimensions you enter, your projects, rooms, estimate items and prices, and your settings (units, currency, theme).

  • Stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.
  • If you turned on Android backup in your phone settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. The app itself sends nothing.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, camera, photos or files.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash-reporting SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising.

Google Play services

  • Google Play Billing — the one-time “BuildPro Pro” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details (Google Privacy Policy).
  • In-app updates and in-app review, provided by the Google Play Store app.

Sharing

Calculation texts and the estimate card image are created on your device and shared only when you tap “Share” and choose where to send them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes
